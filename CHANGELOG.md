# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-28

Split-flap board for the Chrome new tab.

- Hours and minutes, with optional seconds
- 12-hour or 24-hour time, and a timezone
- Date on or off, above or below the cards, with alignment and format
- Background, number, colon, and date colours
- Colon blink
- Flap sound that alternates two recorded strikes
- Settings export and import
- Full-window settings: Clock, Date, Look, and About, with a live preview

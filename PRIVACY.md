# Privacy policy — RetroClocks

**Last updated:** 28 October 2026

This policy applies to the **RetroClocks** Chrome extension by Markus van Kempen.

RetroClocks shows the current time as a split-flap board on the new tab page. It does not require an account and it does not talk to a developer-operated server.

## What we collect

**The developer does not collect, sell, or receive your personal data.**

The extension does not send telemetry, analytics, crash reports, or advertising identifiers. It does not read your browsing history, cookies, or the content of other websites.

## Settings stored on your device

The only permission requested is **`storage`**.

RetroClocks saves your clock preferences locally (and in [Chrome Sync](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/185277) if you have sync turned on):

- 12-hour or 24-hour time
- flap size
- timezone
- whether seconds and the date are shown
- colon blink, date position, date alignment, and date format
- background, number, colon, and date colours
- whether flap sound is on

Flap sounds are two short recordings packaged inside the extension. They are not downloaded.

Those values never leave your browser except through **Google Chrome Sync**, which you control in Chrome’s own settings. The developer cannot see them.

## What we do not do

- No accounts or sign-in
- No ads
- No remote code
- No network requests
- No host permissions
- Time comes from the clock already on your computer

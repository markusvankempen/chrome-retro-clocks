Privacy

Last updated 28 October 2026. Split-Flap Clock by Markus van Kempen.

The extension shows the time on the new tab. It does not require an account and it does not talk to a developer-operated server.

Nothing is collected, sold, or received. There is no telemetry, no ads, and no reading of browsing history or other websites.

The only permission is storage. The clock keeps your preferences on this computer, and in Chrome Sync if you have sync turned on: time format, size, timezone, seconds, colon blink, date, colours, and flap sound. An exported settings file stays where you save it. The flap recordings are packaged inside the extension and are not downloaded.
